# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or technical preferences.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Protect private media, paths, credentials, and diagnostic information.
- Accept correction gracefully and help newcomers reproduce results safely.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release feedback, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

For immediate GitHub platform abuse, use GitHub's **Report abuse** feature. For a repository-specific concern, contact a maintainer privately through a contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive evidence in an issue.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers involved in a report must not participate in deciding its outcome.
